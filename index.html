Intersection Sight Distance (ISD) — AASHTO Green Book Select a control case. ISD defines the required sight triangle leg(s) so drivers have adequate time to see and react to conflicting traffic at an intersection.

Case B — Stop Control on the Minor Road (Departure Sight Triangle) A driver who has stopped on the minor road must be able to see far enough along the major road that the available gap is long enough to complete the maneuver. Sub-cases: B1 left turn, B2 right turn, B3 crossing. ISD = 1.47 × V × tg (US) or 0.278 × V × tg (SI).
