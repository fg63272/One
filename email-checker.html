Email Address Checker

Checks an address against the email standards (RFC 5321 and 5322), catches common domain typos and throwaway inboxes, and looks up the domain's mail servers.

Try:
    Sample list. Paste your own to replace it.
    #AddressResultReason

    What this can't tell you. A correct format and working mail servers mean mail can be delivered to the domain. They don't prove the mailbox exists or that someone reads it. Only a delivered message, such as a confirmation link, proves that.

    The mail server lookup uses Cloudflare's public DNS. Where the page can't reach it, that row says so and the other checks still run.
